How to read this page

  • A lean is a hypothesis with stated reasons, never a certainty.
  • There is no score. Cards show lean, sourced reasons, and what would change the view.
  • Every reason links to its source and shows when it was read.
  • Strategy results appear only after 20 closed paper trades.
  • Charts, heatmap, movers and indicators come from TradingView embeds and are shown as provided. They are not a recommendation.
  • The stock list is S&P 500 membership, which is not a strict ranking by size. Some companies appear twice for two share classes.
  • This page is read only. It never places trades.
